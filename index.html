Minwen Liao

I am currently a research intern at the University of Washington and a third-year undergraduate student at Xinjiang University. I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.

My research goal is to build robots that understand and remember the physical world, autonomously complete complex, long-horizon tasks, and acquire generalizable intelligence by learning from diverse data at scale.

I previously worked on robotic manipulation at Tsinghua AIR.

💬 I am open to discussions and actively seeking collaborators! Feel free to reach out if you're interested in working together on exciting projects.
